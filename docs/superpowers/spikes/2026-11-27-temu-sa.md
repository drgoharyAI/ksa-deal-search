# Spike: temu-sa.search (Temu Saudi, www.temu.com/sa-en, SAR) - 2026-11-27

Status: BLOCKED (login wall for guest search). There is no spec, no fixture (none faked) and no prototype script.

## Saudi storefront
`https://www.temu.com/sa-en/` is the English KSA storefront. The home page title is "Temu Saudi Arabia | ...".
`api/alexa/pc/homepage/activity` returns `language "en"`, `region 174`, `currency "SAR"` and `time_zone "Asia/Riyadh"`.
Search URL: `https://www.temu.com/sa-en/search_result.html?search_key={q}`. The data comes from
`POST /sa-en/api/poppy/v1/search?scene=search`.

The search URL redirected to `login.html` ("Temu - Login"). |
| Same profile: home, 15 s dwell and scroll, then typed "wireless earbuds" into `#searchInput` and pressed Enter | `search_suggest` 403 `{"success":false,"error_code":40001}`, `poppy/v1/search` 403, then a redirect to `login.html?login_scene=2`. |
No CAPTCHA or slider was shown. The block is a forced login (error 40001 and then the login page).

## Conclusion
Temu answers anonymous search with 403/40001 and sends the browser to its login page, even in a real Chrome
window. The brief does not allow account login, so no item data was ever returned. Sort param, discount fields,
timings and bait pricing were not assessed.

## Ideas if revisited (owner decision)

  search would still be risk-gated.
- Category or "deals" pages that do not need search (the home page loads for guests). They are not a search op.
- An affiliate or partner feed.
